## 1. The research question, and why it can score 5/5

**Watch the carrier, not the chat: what a watched LLM cartel changes, and what carries its price.**

When a formed coalition learns it is watched, it changes what it *says*, not what it *charges*, because after formation
the price has two redundant carriers, the sellers' private notes and the market's own quotes, and the chat is not one of
them. Interventions that touch one carrier fail (wipe the agents: 0.91; wipe the market: 0.91; re-instruct an insider to
compete: no change). A capable model under a gag moves the carrier into codes and identical asks (Agrawal's own data). A
trusted agent that offers to collude does not expose the coalition; it manufactures one in sellers who were competing.
So a chat monitor measures compliance theatre; the honest signal is the price against the benchmark plus action-based
plus factors with a null; and the only interventions that work touch both carriers.

Novel in what we searched (`docs/lit/*`): (1) one formed coalition forked into monitor arms, paired, 6 seeds, with a
replay baseline and a told-to-dodge positive control; (2) the carrier 2x2 by resets at the branch (notes vs quotes);
(3) the first test of a trusted agent that offers to collude, with its no-offer control (the organizers list it as
untested); (4) GPT-4.1 under fines and coverage, without a gag, on an emergent coalition (tonight); (5) Agrawal's gag
runs re-analysed for channel movement with a null-calibrated action statistic; (6) unlearning on the 14B with
monitor-coverage labels (supporting). Closest prior: Riemer 2026 (notice only, no formed coalition, no forks).

Impact: a theory of change for monitors (watch the market state and the price, never deploy provocateurs, gags move
the carrier). Execution: paired forks, 6 seeds, positive controls, pre-registered gate, null-calibrated statistics,
two models, verified references, a corrected merge. Presentation: plain English, one claim per section, one figure per claim.

What does not help, and is dropped: a sweeping long-run objective in the prompt (0 of 12 with Qwen); scarcity (2
buyers: price war); new environments; steering. The fear-and-threat report (`docs/lit/fear-threat-inducement.md`) was
read in full. It is useful for exactly one design choice (test T5: a consequence the market enforces, with a twin arm of
the same words and no consequence) and one discussion sentence (pressure is model-dependent: Agrawal's verbal CEO note
forms 10 of 10 on GPT-4.1 and 0 of 3 on Qwen3-14B). Nothing else from it enters the report.
