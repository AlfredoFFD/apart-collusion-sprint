## 3. The smallest useful claim we can support today
> In this market, an established LLM cartel that learns it is watched neither stops nor moves its coordination: with
> six paired seeds, prices stay within 0.03 of the unmonitored fork under every monitor (silent, announced, fined, known
> rotation, random draw, one to three sellers read); telling the sellers changes nothing; fines reduce coordinating
> messages in proportion to how many sellers are read (−0.10, −0.36, −0.48) without reducing collusion, and no fined
> cartel resembles one told to dodge. The price does not depend on the chat, the instruction or any agent's memory:
> fresh agents on both sides, with no view of rivals, continue it from the inherited quotes (0.93) while uninstructed
> agents from scratch reach 0.12–0.43. For monitor design: a message monitor's falling flag count measures its effect
> on the chat, not on the harm.

Supporting block, stated only as far as it goes: fine-tuning a small model on the cartel's logs puts the cartel price
into its weights (it asks the cartel price while told to compete); removing the quarter of turns the budgeted monitor
flagged removed most of that transfer; GRAFT's routing recipe broke the seller at 1.7B; the 14B comparison is pending.
